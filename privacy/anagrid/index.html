Privacy Policy — Anagrid

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Anagrid is a word‑puzzle game published by JokaDev. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: your game stays on your device and there is no account. The app is free and shows ads, served by Google AdMob, which processes some device data to show and measure them. Every puzzle still works without a connection.

Information the app itself collects

None. Anagrid does not send your progress, your word journal, your settings, or anything you type to JokaDev or anyone else. JokaDev has no server and no account system.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app may collect and process your device’s advertising identifier, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), basic device information such as model and operating‑system version, and how you interact with ads (for example, that an ad was shown or tapped). Google processes this data under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices:

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices for ads.
  • US state privacy laws. Where a US state’s law gives you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising (for example, California), you can do so at any time in Settings → Privacy choices for ads.
  • Fewer ads. Settings → Fewer ads turns off banner and full‑screen ads. The only ads left are the ones you choose to watch for a reward, such as a hint.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Information the app stores on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your device’s standard app‑storage. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data:

  • Your game progress — level, experience, coins, streak, equipped skills.
  • Your word journal — words you have solved that the app keeps for you.
  • Your settings — sound, haptics, desk colour, and your ads choices (including a “quiet day” you have earned).

Permissions

Anagrid itself needs no permissions. The advertising software adds the ones it needs to fetch and measure ads: internet and network‑state access, the advertising‑ID permission, Android’s ad‑services permissions (for its privacy‑preserving ad APIs), and permission to run short background tasks (wake lock and foreground service). The puzzles, dictionary and definitions are all built into the app and never need the internet.

Children

Anagrid is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. JokaDev does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device; clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes it. JokaDev holds nothing on a server. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained under Google’s policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the “Last updated” date will change.
