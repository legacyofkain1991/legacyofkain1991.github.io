Privacy Policy — Anagrid

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Anagrid is a word‑puzzle game published by JokaDev. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: the app works fully offline, collects nothing, and has no account.

Information the app collects

None. Anagrid does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device information. It makes no network requests.

Information the app stores on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your device’s standard app‑storage. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data:

  • Your game progress — level, experience, coins, streak, equipped skills.
  • Your word journal — words you have solved that the app keeps for you.
  • Your settings — haptics on/off.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. All puzzles, the dictionary, and word definitions are built into the app and work with no internet connection.

Children

Anagrid is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no information at all, it cannot knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention and deletion

All data stays on your device. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it removes everything. There is no account and nothing is held on a server.

Changes to this policy

A future version of Anagrid may show ads to keep the app free. If that happens, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe the advertising SDK and the data it processes (advertising identifier, approximate location, and app‑interaction data, used for serving and measuring ads). The “Last updated” date will change.
